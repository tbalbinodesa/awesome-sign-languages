# Awesome Sign Languages

A curated, language-specific directory of learning materials, dictionaries, linguistic references, corpora, and software.

Choose the guide language and its corresponding starting point:

| Guide language | Sign language resources presented first |
| --- | --- |
| [Português (Brasil)](README.pt-BR.html) | **Libras** — Língua Brasileira de Sinais |
| [English](README.html) | **ASL** — American Sign Language |

Libras and ASL are distinct natural languages. The language of a guide is not a translation direction, and automatic translation tools do not replace human interpreters.

## About this directory

Resources are included for clear provenance, usefulness, and fit for their stated purpose—not for popularity or search ranking. Check each project's access, data, and reuse terms.

To suggest a resource, follow the contribution guidance in either guide:
[Português](README.pt-BR.html#como-contribuir) or [English](README.html#contributing).
